\# Robust regression engineering demonstration

\#\# Method
I describe the selected implementation as known Huber iteratively reweighted least squares (IRLS), without a novelty claim. For training features X and scalar targets y, form A=[1,X], including an intercept, and initialize beta\_0=argmin\_beta ||y-A beta||\_2\textasciicircum{}2 using numpy.linalg.lstsq with rcond=None. Execute exactly 20 iterations. At iteration t, compute r\_i=y\_i-a\_i\textasciicircum{}T beta\_t and s\_t=max(median\_i |r\_i-median\_j(r\_j)|/0.6744897501960817, 1e-8). Set c\_t=1.345 s\_t and w\_i=1 when |r\_i|<=c\_t, otherwise w\_i=c\_t/|r\_i|. Update beta\_\{t+1\}=argmin\_beta sum\_i w\_i(y\_i-a\_i\textasciicircum{}T beta)\textasciicircum{}2 by least squares on sqrt(w\_i)a\_i and sqrt(w\_i)y\_i. Predict with [1,X\_new] beta\_20. These weights correspond to Huber loss rho(u)=u\textasciicircum{}2/2 for |u|<=1.345 and 1.345|u|-1.345\textasciicircum{}2/2 otherwise, with residual scale recomputed each iteration. The exact selected configuration in snapshots/initial\_full/config.json is method=huber\_irls, iterations=20, delta=1.345, scale=residual\_mad, scale\_floor=1e-8. Selected mean validation MSE is 0.048565701283490234 on subset evaluation and 0.04132739672516235 on full evaluation, both over seeds 0–4. Full OLS baseline validation MSE is 1.99069057153675. Additional validation seeds 10–14 give MSE 0.04058449520270195. The subsequently measured held-out full test MSE is 0.038916122671650406 over seeds 0–4; the test was never used for selection.

\#\# Limitations
This is an application of established robust regression, not a new algorithm. The predictor is linear with an intercept; residual weighting does not establish robustness to high-leverage feature outliers or nonlinear misspecification. The implementation uses a fixed 20 iterations without a convergence check, and recomputing scale does not demonstrate minimization of one fixed-scale Huber objective. Evidence is limited to the supplied benchmark metrics: five primary validation seeds, five additional validation seeds, and five held-out test seeds. These results do not establish real-world generalization, statistical significance, or superiority over unmeasured robust alternatives. Ablation MSEs are 1.99069057153675, 0.07206682832034102, and 0.04132739672516235; their component meanings cannot be inferred from metric files alone. Candidate full-validation MSEs of 0.04135963917671087 and 0.04145129104587781 are close to the selected result, so the small differences should not be presented as decisive without uncertainty analysis. I inspected source, configuration, and recorded metrics read-only; I did not independently reproduce measurements or audit selection chronology.
