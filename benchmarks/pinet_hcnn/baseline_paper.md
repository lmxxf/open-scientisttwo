# Differentiable Hard-Constrained Neural Projection Layers: Predictive Active-Face Newton & Factor-Reused Implicit Adjoints

## 1. Problem Formulation and Uniform Strong Convexity on the Feasible Polytope

Differentiable optimization layers and hard-constrained neural architectures—including OptNet (Amos & Kolter, 2017, `arXiv:1703.00443`), CvxpyLayers (Agrawal et al., 2019, `arXiv:1910.12430`), OSQP operator splitting (Stellato et al., 2020, `arXiv:1711.08013`), DC3 (Donti et al., 2021, `arXiv:2104.12225`), active-set Newton methods on partly smooth manifolds (Lewis & Wylie, `arXiv:1902.00724`), online equality-constrained Newton `OPEN-M` (`arXiv:2212.02748`), black-box active-set QP differentiation `dQP` (Magoon et al., `arXiv:2410.06324`), hard-constrained PINNs (Lu et al., 2021, `arXiv:2102.04626`; `arXiv:2601.06244`), and $\Pi\text{net}$ orthogonal projection layers (Grontas et al., 2026, `arXiv:2508.10480`)—map unconstrained predictions $q_\theta(x)$ onto context-dependent affine equalities and box inequalities:
$$\min_{y \in \mathbb{R}^d} J(y; q) = \frac{1}{2} y^\top Q y + q^\top y + \alpha \sum_{i=1}^d \sin(y_i) \quad \text{s.t.} \quad M y = b, \;\; l \le y \le u$$
where $Q \in \mathbb{S}_{++}^d$ has spectrum in $[0.3, 3.0]$, $M \in \mathbb{R}^{m \times d}$ ($m = d/3$) has row scales spanning up to $10^4\times$, and $\mathcal{B} = [-0.25, 0.25]^d$.

**Theorem 1 (Uniform Strong Convexity & Unique Global KKT Optimum)**:
For $\alpha \in [0, 0.8]$ and $y \in \mathcal{B}$, the Hessian $H(y) = Q - \alpha \operatorname{diag}(\sin(y))$ satisfies $\lambda_{\min}(H(y)) \ge 0.3 - 0.8 \sin(0.25) = 0.102077 > 0$. Thus $J(y; q)$ is uniformly $\mu$-strongly convex on $\mathcal{F} = \{y \in \mathcal{B} : My = b\}$, guaranteeing a unique global minimizer $y^\star(q)$ characterized by primal feasibility, complementary slackness, and dual stationarity $\nabla J(y^\star) + M^\top \lambda^\star + \nu^\star = 0$.

## 2. Predictive Nullspace Active-Face Newton & Retained-Cholesky Implicit Adjoints

1. **Contrast with $\Pi\text{net}$ (`2508.10480`), `dQP` (`2410.06324`), and Classical Active-Set Newton (`1902.00724`, `2212.02748`)**:
   - $\Pi\text{net}$ relies on first-order Douglas–Rachford/ADMM operator splitting, which converges only linearly and leaves $10^{-4}\text{--}10^{-3}$ suboptimality at fixed iteration budgets.
   - `dQP` (`arXiv:2410.06324`) decouples forward QP solving from backward differentiation by forming and factorizing a reduced active-set KKT system post-hoc for pure quadratic objectives ($\alpha = 0$).
   - By contrast, our layer handles **non-quadratic convex objectives ($\alpha > 0$)** and **couples the forward solver directly with the backward pass**: it eliminates $My = b$ via orthonormal nullspace parameterization $y = y_p + V w$, uses a 3-step projected gradient predictor to jump across multiple bound activations simultaneously, maintains the reduced face tangent basis $Z_A = V B_A \in \mathbb{R}^{d \times k}$ via fast $O(|A|^2 n)$ QR/Householder updates, and retains the terminal forward Cholesky factor $L_A L_A^\top = Z_A^\top H(y^\star) Z_A$ so that backward vector-Jacobian products (VJPs) require **zero matrix reconstruction and zero refactorization**!
2. **Theorem 2 (Exact Stable-Face Implicit Adjoint with $O(dk + k^2)$ Retained-Factor VJP Complexity)**:
   - On a locally stable active face $A$ (under strict complementary slackness), differentiating $Z_A^\top \nabla J(y^\star(q); q) = 0$ yields:
     $$\frac{\partial y^\star}{\partial q} = -Z_A (Z_A^\top H(y^\star) Z_A)^{-1} Z_A^\top = -Z_A L_A^{-\top} L_A^{-1} Z_A^\top, \qquad \operatorname{VJP}(v) = \Bigl(\frac{\partial y^\star}{\partial q}\Bigr)^\top v = -Z_A L_A^{-\top} \bigl(L_A^{-1} (Z_A^\top v)\bigr).$$
   - Reusing the cached forward pair $(Z_A, L_A)$ computes any backward VJP in strict $O(d k + k^2)$ floating-point operations (two matrix-vector products of size $d \times k$ and two $k \times k$ triangular back-substitutions), avoiding $O(d k^2 + k^3)$ Hessian reconstruction and Cholesky refactorization.
