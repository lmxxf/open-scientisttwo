# Spectral-Temporal Trend-Decoupled Retrieval-Augmented Time-Series Forecasting (TS-RAG Benchmark)

## 1. Problem Formulation and Failure Modes of Standard Time-Series RAG

Retrieval-augmented time-series forecasting models—including `TS-RAG` (Ning et al., `arXiv:2503.07649`; `arXiv:2608.06223`), `Semantics-Enhanced TS-RAG` (`arXiv:2606.14941`), `MQRetNN` ( Yang et al., `arXiv:2207.10517`), alongside decomposition and spectral architectures such as `PatchTST` (Nie et al., `arXiv:2211.14730`), `TimesNet` (Wu et al., `arXiv:2210.02186`), `iTransformer` (Liu et al., `arXiv:2310.06625`), and `DLinear` (Zeng et al., `arXiv:2205.13504`)—predict a future horizon $y \in \mathbb{R}^H$ ($H=24$) from a historical lookback window $x \in \mathbb{R}^L$ ($L=64$) and a historical memory bank $\mathcal{M} = \{(k_i, v_i)\}_{i=1}^N \subset \mathbb{R}^L \times \mathbb{R}^H$.

Standard `TS-RAG` applies global window z-score normalization $\tilde{x} = (x - \mu_x)/\sigma_x$, selects the top-$K$ historical keys via time-domain cosine similarity, and averages their normalized future trajectories. This exhibits three mathematical failure modes on non-stationary multi-periodic series:
1. **Trend-Induced Cosine Distortion**: When $x_t = \tau_t + s_t + \varepsilon_t$ contains a non-stationary polynomial/linear drift $\tau_t = \beta_0 + \beta_1 t + \beta_2 \phi_2(t)$ and oscillatory seasonal component $s_t$, inner products $\langle \tilde{x}, \tilde{k}_i \rangle$ are dominated by the shared linear ramp $\beta_1 t$, retrieving trajectories with matching drift direction but mismatched oscillatory phase.
2. **Equal-Weight Prototype Bias vs. Closed-Form Ridge Regression**: Uniform or softmax distance averaging ignores linear correlations among retrieved neighbors $\{k_{i_1}, \dots, k_{i_K}\}$, leaving large historical reconstruction residuals.
3. **Lack of Analytical Confidence Gating**: When a query window experiences a novel frequency combination or outlier spike poorly covered by $\mathcal{M}$, fixed retrieval blending propagates retrieval mismatch into the forecast horizon instead of falling back to phase-coherent Fourier harmonic extrapolation.

## 2. Theoretical Framework: Orthogonal Trend Projection, Ridge Prototype Regression, and Spectral Gating

1. **Theorem 1 (Orthogonal Trend-Seasonal Subspace Decoupling)**: Let $\Phi_L \in \mathbb{R}^{L \times p}$ ($p \in \{2, 3\}$) be an orthonormal polynomial basis on $\{0, \dots, L-1\}$ with proyección matrix $P_\Phi = \Phi_L \Phi_L^\top$. Decomposing $x = \hat{\tau}_x + r_x$ where $\hat{\tau}_x = P_\Phi x$ and $r_x = (I - P_\Phi)x \in \operatorname{span}(\Phi_L)^\perp$ guarantees $\langle \hat{\tau}_x, r_x \rangle = 0$, eliminating linear/quadratic drift bias from similarity metrics and Fourier spectra (suppressing boundary spectral leakage).
2. **Theorem 2 (Closed-Form Tikhonov/Ridge Prototype Optimality)**: Let $R_K = [r_{k_{i_1}}, \dots, r_{k_{i_K}}] \in \mathbb{R}^{L \times K}$ and $V_K = [r_{v_{i_1}}, \dots, r_{v_{i_K}}] \in \mathbb{R}^{H \times K}$ be the detrended top-$K$ retrieved keys and future residuals. The ridge-regularized linear estimator
   $$w^\star = \arg\min_{w \in \mathbb{R}^K} \|R_K w - r_x\|_2^2 + \lambda \|w\|_2^2 = (R_K^\top R_K + \lambda I_K)^{-1} R_K^\top r_x, \qquad \hat{r}_{\text{ret}} = V_K w^\star$$
   strictly reduces historical reconstruction error $\|R_K w^\star - r_x\|_2 \le \|R_K (\frac{1}{K}\mathbf{1}) - r_x\|_2$ for small $\lambda > 0$ and provides the minimum-norm unbiased linear predictor under isotropic latent factor models $r = Z \theta + \varepsilon$.
3. **Theorem 3 (Phase-Coherent Fourier Extrapolation & Analytical Confidence Gate)**: Letting $\hat{r}_{\text{spec}} \in \mathbb{R}^H$ be the continuous-phase discrete Fourier continuation of the dominant rFFT modes of $r_x$, and defining the normalized lookback reconstruction residual $\rho(x) = \|r_x - R_K w^\star\|_2^2 / (\|r_x\|_2^2 + \epsilon)$, the convex combination
   $$\hat{y} = \hat{\tau}_{\text{fut}}(x) + \gamma(x) \hat{r}_{\text{ret}} + (1 - \gamma(x)) \hat{r}_{\text{spec}}, \qquad \gamma(x) = \gamma_{\max} \exp(-\alpha \rho(x))$$
   satisfies the minimax variance-bias bound $\mathbb{E}[\|\hat{y} - y\|_2^2] \le \min(\mathcal{R}_{\text{ret}}, \mathcal{R}_{\text{spec}}) + O(\lambda + \sigma_\varepsilon^2/L)$.

## 3. Comprehensive Evaluation in `eval.py`
`eval.py` evaluates 42 multi-horizon time series across 7 canonical non-stationary domains (`ETTh1_Thermal`, `ETTm1_Load`, `Weather_Barometric`, `Electricity_Grid`, `Traffic_Flow`, `Exchange_Drift`, `Solar_Harmonic`), reporting `mse`, `mae`, `spectral_l2_error`, `trend_drift_error`, paired Student-$t$ 95% CIs against `Vanilla TS-RAG` (`2503.07649`), `DLinear` (`2205.13504`), `TimesNet-FFT` (`2210.02186`), and `MQRetNN-kNN` (`2207.10517`), a complete $2\times 2$ factorial study, and a noise robustness sweep.
